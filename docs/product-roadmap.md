# Product roadmap

FastHireX is a final year project in active development. This page gives a plain view of what the first version covers and where it is headed.

## v1 scope

The first version brings together the core loop end to end:

- **Verified FAST-only signup**, the gate that makes everything else trustworthy.
- **Skill evaluation**, reading real work into an evidence-backed profile.
- **Roadmaps and prep**, field-based content, goal checklists, streaks, and a leaderboard.
- **Communities**, auto-joined course channels, project spaces, direct messages, and calls.
- **Jobs and hiring**, a two-sided marketplace from applied to hired, with reviews and reputation.
- **Community feed and alumni spotlight.**

## Guiding principles

- **Proof over promises.** Every claim on the platform should point at the work behind it.
- **Two-sided from day one.** A feature should help the student and the employer, not just one side.
- **Honest by default.** The product would rather show an honest, modest result than an impressive one it cannot defend.

## Beyond FAST

The product is designed to scale past FAST-NUCES to other universities. The name is built for it: "FAST" reframes to mean *fast*, speed, as the platform grows, with no rename needed.
